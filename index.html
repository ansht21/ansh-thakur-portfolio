Supply Chain Inventory & Performance Optimization

Cleaned and analyzed 7,991 orders across 47 SKUs to find out whether late deliveries were a warehouse-specific problem or a systemic one — and to prioritize inventory attention using ABC classification.

PythonPandasSQL / MySQL Excel / VBAPower BI · DAX

Key Findings

  • Delivery-time variance between warehouses is just 0.08% — the late-delivery issue is systemic, not concentrated in one site.
  • ABC classification shows 78.7% of revenue comes from A-tier SKUs, out of 47 total.
  • Full pipeline analyzed $82.7M in revenue and $30.9M in profit across 7,991 orders.
  • Excel/VBA dashboard automates the reporting layer; Power BI/DAX used for supporting analysis views.

Ride Demand Forecasting & Revenue Analytics

Analyzed 35,000+ ride records to understand when demand peaks and which trip types actually drive revenue — cleaning the data in SQL before building out the demand and fare analysis in Python and Power BI.

PythonSQL / MySQLPower BI ExcelPandasMatplotlib / Seaborn

Key Findings

  • 5–7 PM carries 31.2% of total demand — the clearest single window for supply planning.
  • Airport trips are 39.6% of ride volume but drive 54.8% of revenue.
  • Fare-per-km holds steady around ₹22.06–₹22.41 once distance is controlled for.
  • SQL cleaning used window functions and ROW_NUMBER() deduplication ahead of the Python EDA and Power BI build.
