Supply Chain Performance Analysis

Business problem: Half of all orders arrive late and $41M of revenue is exposed. Is that one bad warehouse — or the entire network?

50.5% On-time rate (≤5 days target)
$41.0M Revenue on late orders
0.08% Variance between warehouses
37.4% Average profit margin

The answer surprised the brief: no single warehouse is at fault. Only 0.08% of delivery-time variance sits between sites — the lateness is systemic, not localisable. NMK1003 tops the raw late-order count because it handles 31.4% of all volume, not because it is slow. A per-site improvement project would have fixed nothing.

Uber Trip Demand Analysis

Business problem: When should a ride-hailing operator surge-price, where should premium vehicles be positioned, and which fleet tier to grow? 35,000 trip records, 8 business questions answered.

31.2% Demand in just 5–7 PM
54.8% Revenue from airport trips
2.8× Uber Black vs Pool per km
3 of 8 Findings are "don't act"

Three of the eight findings tell the business not to act: no weekend fare effect (0.9% gap), no seasonal cycle (11% month variance), and no valid "top zones" strategy (4 of 5 zones needed to reach 80% of revenue). Knowing where not to spend is part of the job.

Analytics Workflow

How I approach every data project — from business question to recommendation.

  1. 1

    Business Problem

    Define the specific question the business needs answered. Avoid starting with the data.

  2. 2

    Data Collection & Inspection

    Load raw data, audit nulls, check data types, and understand what each field means.

  3. 3

    Data Cleaning & Engineering

    Remove duplicates, handle missing values, standardise formats, calculate derived metrics.

  4. 4

    SQL / Python Analysis

    Run aggregations, window functions, CTEs, and Python EDA to surface patterns.

  5. 5

    Visualisation & Dashboard

    Build Power BI or Excel dashboards that communicate findings to non-technical readers.

  6. 6

    KPI Interpretation

    Distinguish reliable findings from noise, and report negative results honestly.

  7. 7

    Business Recommendation

    Deliver clear, evidence-backed actions — including where the data says not to act.
