* **Confidence-Gated GenAI Operations Copilot:** Deployed on Hugging Face Spaces where the SARIMA 95% confidence band gates an LLM (Groq) writing daily operations briefs—preventing hallucinations by triggering AI strictly on statistically confirmed demand anomalies.

### 2. ⚡ [E-Commerce Revenue Reconciliation & Leakage Audit Engine](https://gitlab.com/ansht_121/data-analytics/-/tree/main/10_ecommerce-reconciliation-engine)
> **May 2026 – Jun 2026** · *Python, SQL, Isolation Forest, Gradient Boosting, LangChain (RAG), ChromaDB, Streamlit, Excel, pytest, GitLab CI*

![Revenue Cockpit](images/revenue-cockpit.webp)

* **128,976 Amazon Orders Audited (₹7.86 Cr Gross GMV):** Engineered an 11-step cleaning and financial reconciliation pipeline classifying every order into 6 financial buckets (*Net Realized, In-Transit, Pending, Cancellation, Return, Loss*) across 14 fiscal weeks.
* **Forensic Revenue Leakage Separation:** Separated true operational revenue loss (**₹83.07 Lakhs**, 83.3% driven by 18,334 order cancellations) from **₹5.10 Crore capital actively in transit**, replacing an erroneous 23.7% gross-coverage metric with boardroom-accurate figures.
* **Dual Financial Cockpits:** Delivered a formula-driven Microsoft Excel dashboard (6-KPI wall with 5 dropdown slicers) alongside an interactive Streamlit application with an ML risk radar and one-click dispute ticket generator.
* **ML Anomaly & Policy RAG Copilot:** Deployed an Isolation Forest detecting **2,215 irregular settlement records** plus a Gradient-Boosting leakage classifier reaching **0.9235 ROC-AUC**, paired with a ChromaDB RAG copilot that automatically drafts Amazon Seller Support claims citing the exact applicable policy clauses.
* **CI/CD Quality Guard:** Enforced through **10 passing pytest tests** with seed-fixed reproducibility, Ruff linting, Makefile automation, and GitLab CI on every commit.
